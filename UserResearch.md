## What we found

From the sources
- 55 % of university students who use AI say it makes them think less deeply (Inside Higher Ed, 2025) and 67 % of students believe it harms their critical thinking (RAND, 2026).
- In Mexico, people who don't read say it is because of lack of interest (34.6 %) or lack of time (32.4 %) (INEGI, MOLEC 2025).
- A habit takes 66 days on average to form, and missing one day doesn't break it (Lally et al., 2010).
- Answering questions about what you read helps you remember it more than rereading (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006).
- People understand better reading on paper than on a screen (Delgado et al., 2018).

## Needs
1. Reading should be easy to start even when I have little time.
2. Missing a day shouldn't make me give up on everything.
3. My phone shouldn't interrupt me while I read.
4. I want to remember what I read, not just turn pages.
5. Technology should help me think, not think for me.

## Customer journey

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## How this changed the app
- We replaced the streak with reading momentum because missing a day doesn't break a habit (Lally).
- The app asks you to close it because people understand better on paper and the phone is the main distraction.
- We added quizzes because answering questions helps you remember.
- AI doesn't write anything for the user because that is exactly what worries students.
